How this model works

Every stage credits a clear reward plus a fixed amount of idle gold. Rewards grow geometrically every ten stages. Available gold buys upgrades sequentially at escalating prices (maximum 100 upgrades). All upgrades increase unit base HP and attack by linear increments. Tank, damage and support roles use fixed illustrative HP and attack multipliers (tank 1.8 / 0.55, damage 0.8 / 1.6, support 1.0 / 0.6). Attack interval is 1 second; team DPS is the sum of role attack. There are no healing or skill effects.

Enemy HP and enemy DPS are authored independently from player strength, using the chosen enemy growth per ten stages. Each wave is identical. Estimated clear time = total wave HP / team DPS. Survival time = team HP / enemy DPS; margin = survival time / clear time. Margin below 1 means estimated HP is insufficient. This is a deterministic approximation with no movement, targeting, RNG, mitigation, skill timing or combat playback. Rewards are credited on schedule regardless of outcomes so designers can inspect planned progression even after a flagged stage; this is not a prediction of player behavior or win rate.

All figures and scenarios are fictional, unrelated to production game data. The separate public Economy Workbench, Unit Benchmark and Progression Estimator remain available for their original demo export/import workflow.
