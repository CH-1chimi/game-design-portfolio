Player team CP by stage (1–120)

Demo logic: reward and summon pricing use the imported public economy assumptions. Level = min(50, 1 + floor(stage/4) + min(10, floor(affordable draws/3))). Team CP = example roster size × (0.15×levelled HP + 3×levelled ATK). Enemy target = team CP × target ratio. No battle win rates, AI behavior, troop counts, lineups or game economy are simulated.
